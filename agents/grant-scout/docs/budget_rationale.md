# How the grant budget is split, and why

The drafter starts every budget from `grant_scout/budget.py`, which splits the award by
the brand's **stage**, then adapts it to each grant's rules. This note explains the choices
so Nova can check them before submitting.

## What reviewers reward

- **Specific, quantified line items.** Small-business grant rubrics score budget clarity
  and specificity of the use of funds on its own (e.g. up to 10 points in one scoring guide),
  and top-scoring examples name concrete purchases, such as "our first 2,000 branded packages".
- **Costs tied to outcomes.** Rubrics ask whether costs are "linked directly to activities
  and outcomes" and whether the budget is "reasonable" for the project's goals.
- **A complete budget.** New designers often budget for samples and forget grading,
  packaging and shipping, so every plan keeps an operations and contingency line.

## House of Nova's stage: growth (post-launch)

House of Nova debuted at NYFW in September 2025 and is stocked at DOORS NYC. The next step a
funder wants to see is **turning that attention into revenue and a repeatable pipeline**. The
plan for this stage, scaled to the award:

| Area | Share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Capsule production of proven runway looks | 35% | Revenue-generating, visible result of the grant |
| Next-collection development and sampling | 20% | Shows a pipeline past the debut. Plan for 2-3 sample rounds |
| Wholesale and sales development | 15% | Showroom or market-week fee, line sheets, commission-based agent |
| Marketing, content and PR | 15% | Product photography, targeted paid social, press seeding |
| Digital and innovation | 7% | Funds the AI and phygital side that makes the label distinctive |
| Operations and contingency | 8% | Packaging, shipping, insurance, overruns |

**Judgement calls:**
- **Sales staff:** a salaried hire isn't recommended yet. Sales agents usually work for 10-15%
  commission, and market-week or showroom fees cost far less than a salary. Many grants also
  exclude payroll. A part-time, fixed-term sales and operations role is added only for awards
  of $50,000 or more, and only where the grant allows payroll.
- **Small awards (under $5,000):** the money goes to the two most important areas plus
  contingency, because one clear outcome reads better than six tiny lines.
- **Each grant's focus wins.** A sustainability grant shifts money toward materials. An
  innovation grant shifts it toward the digital line.

## Cost benchmarks (estimates to confirm with quotes)

- Sampling: about $2,600-$10,200 for a 4-style collection. Complex pieces can cost $600-$2,500 each.
- First small-batch production run: about $3,000-$15,000.
- Launch marketing: about $1,000-$5,000.
- First market week: about $3,000-$8,000 in total. Juried emerging-designer sections are cheaper.
